## Life~Work
I stayed busy looking for my next full-time role. I've been picky about what I consider, so I've had a good hit rate for interviews. It's been fun (and a little stressful) to talk with so many recruiters, hiring managers, and teams. My aim in every conversation is to interview them as much as they're interviewing me. As I write this, I have two strong opportunities in progress, and I am still open to hearing about product design roles that fit my profile. I'm focused on companies with a positive mission, with teams building marketplaces and rethinking their workflows and tooling. If that's you (or you know anyone), [reach out](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kylejohnston/).

## Projects
I focused on one side project: [MEMEX](https://memex.kyleio.com), aka my swipe file, aka my collection of visual inspiration. I migrated it to Astro, the same framework that powers this journal. When it launched in 2018, MEMEX was built with PHP, using a simple command to render a folder full of images on a page. I loved the simplicity, but PHP limited my hosting options and organizing and syncing new visuals was always a drag. Claude helped with the migration and created scripts that automate organizing and uploading. And running on Astro means I can host almost anywhere, so I moved it from DigitalOcean to Cloudflare, and it now lives at [memex.kyleio.com](https://memex.kyleio.com).

## Digital gardening

### Bookshelf
- [*The Buried Giant* by Kazuo Ishiguro](https://books.kyleio.com/book/buried-giant/) – a dreamy, fantasy novel set in a time of knights and dragons, with themes of memory and loss emerging through a fog that's enveloped the land.  
- [*Helen of Nowhere* by Makenna Goodman](https://books.kyleio.com/book/helen-of-nowhere/) – a short, surreal read touching on the past and present, regret and happiness. 
- [*Box 88* by Charles Cumming](https://books.kyleio.com/book/box-88/) – a change of pace: a modern spy thriller. It's the first in a series I found through the [Why Is This Interesting?](https://whyisthisinteresting.substack.com/p/the-friday-interview-with-charles) newsletter, and I'm looking forward to reading more of the series. 

### Links

- [Benchma(r)king – James Bridle](https://emergencemagazine.org/essay/benchmarking/) — Great read for two reasons. First, I learned the origin of 'benchmarking' — not what I expected! Second, seeing the results of his attempts to build a chair designed by AI, or "designed by someone who has read a lot about chairs but never actually sat in one"
- [Preview a Link](https://previewa.link/) – Handy tool from [Generic Applications](https://generic-applications.com/) for checking link previews across a wide range of applications and platforms.
