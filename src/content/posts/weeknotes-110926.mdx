Trying this format to get in the groove of writing more often. Capping each section at 5 items to keep it easy.

## Things I did

* Continuing my hunt for a new product design role, I interviewed with two companies. The first company was the final round of interviews—four meetings across two days, focused on a case study I developed for them. For the second company, I presented two projects from my portfolio to a cross-functional panel. I should hear their decisions in the week ahead. 🤞
* Worked on finalizing a new landing page for [HNC Living](https://hncliving.org/), a non-profit I’ve been working with since 2017. They provide resources and financial support to head and neck cancer patients, and they’re launching a new program that builds partnerships with dental offices. 
* Trying [TK](https://tk.xyz/), the new writing app from Medium. The recommendation came from an Etsy alum who’s building it. I _really_ like the writing experience so far; there are a range of formatting and editing tools that stay out of the way until you need them, and it works in the browser, iOS, and iPadOS.
* Started prototyping a concept for a non-deterministic UI/UX. Think: a search interface that adapts to how someone’s needs might change as they move through an experience of searching, chatting, tracking things that get created, etc. More on this in a separate post.
* This site: finished a [project recap for mage](https://kyleio.com/p/project-mage/), my macOS command-line tool for resizing and optimizing images. Also added support for threaded posts, inspired by [Marcel’s blog](https://marcel.io/posts/it-s-so-tiny).

## Things I read

Currently reading two books, and I have mixed feelings about both:

* _Moby Dick,_ the classic by Herman Melville. It has a lighter tone than I expected. When I pick it up, I enjoy reading it. But I find myself hesitant to dive in. Might be the flowery 19th century language (I’m learning old words for common ideas) or the pace (22 chapters before they set sail).
* _Weak in Comparison to Dreams_, an experimental novel by James Elkins, who’s an art historian and critic. So far, very strange. I’m 1/3 of the way through it, but I haven’t decided how I feel about it or if I’m going to finish it now or come back to it in the future.
